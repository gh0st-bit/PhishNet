## Product proposition

PhishNet combines phishing-awareness simulations, learning content and management visibility in an organization-scoped application. The current local lab lets a prospect watch a complete campaign produce measurable behavior without configuring an external email provider. This is a proposed positioning statement, not validated market demand.

## Three-minute demonstration

1. Manager opens the dashboard: show assigned-team outcomes, learning coverage and permitted follow-up. An OrgAdmin opens organization campaign reports.
2. Operator creates a local practice campaign by choosing recipients. Show successful queue delivery in the inbox.
3. Employee opens and clicks the message, completes the training-code page, and downloads the harmless attachment.
4. A second recipient reports the message. Open campaign results and Reports to show both risky and positive behavior with actual persisted counts.
5. Export the overview CSV. Show that an employee cannot open management data and another organization cannot see the campaign.

## Claims supported by this build

Working local simulation outcomes, dedicated management views, organization/role boundaries exercised by automated checks, content workflows, persisted data checks and exportable overview metrics. These demonstrate software behavior in the tested local environment.

## Claims that still require evidence

Do not claim paying customers, revenue, retention, validated behavior change, market share, certified compliance, global service reliability or enterprise integration readiness without independent evidence. No pricing, TAM or revenue projection was invented for this increment.

Before an external pilot, assign owners and acceptance criteria for production hosting/HTTPS, backup recovery, mail deliverability, security and privacy review, retention, support and incident handling. Before pitching scale, obtain design-partner feedback and measure activation, setup time, completion, reporting and repeat behavior over time. Agree measurable targets with the pilot customer rather than presenting invented success numbers.
